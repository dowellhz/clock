快速开始Quick Start

切换时钟Switch Clocks

滑动屏幕在十二种时钟之间切换；轻触屏幕，可以换同一种时钟的不同造型。Swipe the screen to switch between twelve clocks; tap to change the look of the current clock.

屏幕方向Screen Orientation

iPhone 上为横屏显示，请将设备横向放置；iPad 横屏和竖屏都可以使用。On iPhone, the app displays in landscape, so hold your device horizontally. On iPad, you can use either landscape or portrait.

动画设置Animation Settings

开启系统“减少动态效果”后，部分分钟切换动画会缩短或直接完成。When the system’s Reduce Motion setting is enabled, some minute-transition animations are shortened or completed immediately.

保持常亮Keep the Screen On

App 位于前台时会请求保持屏幕常亮；切换到其他 App 后恢复系统默认行为。While in the foreground, the app requests that the screen stay on. Switching to another app restores the system’s default behavior.

常见问题Frequently Asked Questions

为什么时间没有立即变化？Why Does the Time Not Change Immediately?

部分场景会在整分钟到达后播放数秒动画。数字会随着车辆、蚂蚁、方块或鱼群的动作逐步完成。Some scenes play an animation for a few seconds when a new minute begins. The digits gradually take shape as vehicles, ants, blocks, or schools of fish move.

需要联网吗？Does It Need an Internet Connection?

不需要。无事钟使用设备本地时间，并在设备上完成所有渲染。No. needless clock uses your device’s local time and renders everything on the device.

支持竖屏吗？Is Portrait Orientation Supported?

iPad 支持竖屏；iPhone 与 Apple TV 为横屏显示。iPad supports portrait orientation; iPhone and Apple TV display in landscape.

支持哪些设备？Which Devices Are Supported?

支持能够运行 iOS 17 或 iPadOS 17 及更高版本的 iPhone 和 iPad，以及 Mac 与 Apple TV。在 Apple TV 上，用遥控器上下滑动切换时钟、左右滑动切换造型，按播放/暂停键打开设置。The app supports iPhone and iPad running iOS 17 or iPadOS 17 or later, as well as Mac and Apple TV. On Apple TV, swipe up or down on the remote to switch clocks, swipe left or right to change the look, and press Play/Pause to open settings.

联系与问题反馈Contact and Feedback

如果问题仍未解决，请发送邮件并附上设备型号、系统版本、App 版本和问题复现步骤，但不要提交个人或敏感信息。If the issue remains unresolved, please email your device model, OS version, app version, and steps to reproduce the issue. Do not include personal or sensitive information.
