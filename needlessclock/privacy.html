隐私政策Privacy Policy

无事钟的核心原则很简单：一个时钟不需要知道你是谁。App 的时钟体验在设备本地完成。The principle behind needless clock is simple: a clock does not need to know who you are. The app’s clock experience runs locally on your device.

摘要Summary

无事钟不要求创建账号，不包含广告或第三方分析 SDK，也不会收集、存储、出售或共享你的个人数据。needless clock does not require an account, contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs, and does not collect, store, sell, or share your personal data.

App 使用的信息Information the App Uses

App 会在设备上读取当前日期、时间、时区以及“减少动态效果”等系统显示偏好，用于正确展示时钟与动画。这些信息仅在本地处理，不会由无事钟上传到服务器。The app reads the current date, time, time zone, and system display preferences such as Reduce Motion on your device to display the clock and animations correctly. This information is processed locally only and is not uploaded to a server by needless clock.
