常见问题

如何切换城市？
轻点任意一侧时钟，搜索并选择城市、大学或高中。

为什么无法获取位置？
请在“设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 定位服务”中允许双城时钟使用位置。你也可以随时手动选择城市。

没有网络还能使用吗？
可以。时钟与本地城市搜索保持可用；天气和在线地点搜索需要网络连接。

支持哪些设备？
双城时钟支持运行 iOS 或 iPadOS 17 及以上版本的 iPhone 和 iPad。

Frequently asked questions

How do I change a city?
Tap either clock, then search for and select a city, university, or high school.

Why can't the app find my location?
Allow location access for Dual Flip Clock in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services. You can always choose a city manually.

Does it work offline?
Yes. Clocks and local city search remain available; weather and online place search require a connection.

Which devices are supported?
Dual Flip Clock supports iPhone and iPad running iOS or iPadOS 17 or later.
