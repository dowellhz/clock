隐私政策Privacy Policy

更新日期：2026 年 8 月 30 日Last updated: August 30, 2026

我们不收集您的个人数据

DualFlipClock 不要求注册账户，不包含广告或第三方跟踪，也不会由开发者收集、存储或出售您的个人信息。

位置

只有当您主动启用 GPS 时钟并授权位置权限后，App 才会在设备上使用您的位置，用于确定当地城市、时区与天气。拒绝位置权限不影响手动选择城市。位置信息不会发送给开发者。

天气与地点查询

为显示天气，App 会向 Open-Meteo 发送所选地点的经纬度。在线搜索地点时，查询文字会发送至 Open-Meteo 地理编码服务；GPS 反向地理编码由 Apple 的系统服务处理。这些服务按各自的隐私政策处理请求。

本地存储

您选择的城市、显示偏好与搜索历史保存在设备本地，用于改善使用体验。删除 App 会一并移除这些本地数据。

联系我们

如对本政策有疑问，请通过支持页面联系我们。

We do not collect your personal data

DualFlipClock requires no account, contains no advertising or third-party tracking, and does not collect, store, or sell your personal information.

Location

Only when you enable the GPS clock and grant permission does the app use your location on-device to determine the local city, time zone, and weather. You can always select a city manually. Your location is not sent to the developer.

Weather and place lookup

To show weather, the app sends the selected place's coordinates to Open-Meteo. Online place queries are sent to Open-Meteo's geocoding service, while GPS reverse geocoding is handled by Apple's system service. These services process requests under their own privacy policies.

Local storage

Your selected cities, display preferences, and search history are stored locally on your device to improve your experience. Removing the app also removes this local data.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please use our support page.
