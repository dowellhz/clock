帮助与支持Help & Support

如何切换皮肤？How do I change skins?

轻点左上角画笔图标，选择皮肤后点击“完成”。选择会保存在本机。Tap the brush icon, choose a skin, then tap Done. Your choice is saved on this device.

如何添加小组件？How do I add a widget?

长按主屏幕空白处，选择编辑并添加小组件，搜索安装后的 App。支持的尺寸取决于所选布局。秒数跟随设置中的“显示秒数”；系统可能降低秒数显示频率。Touch and hold an empty area of the Home Screen, then use Edit to add a widget and find the installed app. Sizes vary by layout. Seconds follow Show seconds in Settings; iOS may reduce how often seconds are displayed.

天气为什么不可用？Why is weather unavailable?

天气需要网络与使用期间定位权限。可在系统设置中允许定位，也可在 App 设置中关闭天气；时钟和计时器仍可离线使用。Weather needs an internet connection and location permission while using the app. Allow location in system Settings or turn weather off in the app. The clock and timers work offline.

锁屏实时活动能一直显示吗？Will Live Activities stay visible indefinitely?

实时活动由 iOS 管理，可能因系统限制而结束。锁屏实时钟到期后需要重新开启。计时提醒需要通知权限。iOS manages Live Activities and may end them due to system limits. Restart the Lock Screen clock when it expires. Timer notifications require permission.

夜间变暗使用哪个时区？Which time zone controls night dimming?

夜间变暗和桌面小组件使用设备本地时区；手动时区仅影响 App 主时钟。Night dimming and Home Screen widgets use the device’s local time zone. A manual time zone affects only the main app clock.
