概述Overview

适用于简刻 PlainTick Flip Clock（com.linlu.simpleclock）。App 无需账号，不含广告、第三方分析 SDK 或跨 App 跟踪功能。This policy applies to PlainTick Flip Clock (com.linlu.simpleclock). The app requires no account and includes no advertising, third-party analytics SDKs or cross-app tracking.

本地数据Local data

皮肤、显示偏好、时区、闹钟及计时状态保存在设备上。App 与小组件通过本机 App Group 共享设置；不会将这些设置上传到开发者服务器。卸载 App 会移除本地 App 数据。Skins, display preferences, time zones, alarms and timer state are stored on your device. The app and its widgets share settings through a local App Group. These settings are not uploaded to a developer server. Uninstalling the app removes local app data.

可选天气与位置Optional weather and location

开启天气时，App 经你的许可请求当前位置，以约三公里精度定位，并将四舍五入到两位小数的经纬度通过 HTTPS 发送给 Open-Meteo，以获取天气。不会持续追踪位置，也不会向开发者服务器上传位置。网络请求也会向天气服务暴露 IP 地址等必要连接信息。你可以关闭天气或撤销定位权限。When weather is enabled, the app requests your current location with permission, using approximately three-kilometre accuracy. Latitude and longitude rounded to two decimal places are sent to Open-Meteo over HTTPS to retrieve weather. The app does not continuously track location or upload location to a developer server. Network requests also disclose connection information such as your IP address to the weather service. You can disable weather or revoke location permission.

第三方服务Third-party services

天气服务的数据处理规则请参阅 Open-Meteo 条款与隐私说明。App Store、系统通知和实时活动由 Apple 提供；参阅 Apple 隐私政策。See Open-Meteo’s terms and privacy information for the weather service’s practices. Apple provides the App Store, system notifications and Live Activities; see Apple’s privacy policy.

权限Permissions

定位仅用于可选天气；通知仅用于你启用的闹钟、整点报时或计时提醒。拒绝这些权限不影响主时钟的使用。Location is used only for optional weather. Notifications are used for alarms, hourly chimes or timer reminders you enable. Declining these permissions does not prevent use of the main clock.

支持邮件与网站Support email and website

发送支持邮件时，我们会使用你自愿提供的邮箱及问题内容来回复。网站由 GitHub Pages 托管，GitHub 可能处理访问日志。本网站仅在浏览器本地保存语言偏好，不接入广告或分析脚本。If you email support, we use the email address and information you voluntarily provide to respond. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages, whose provider may process access logs. The site stores only a language preference in your browser and includes no advertising or analytics scripts.
